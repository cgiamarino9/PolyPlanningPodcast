Episode 1 · Fall 2025

Interview with Christopher Pina, Community Planner (City of LA)

Chase Abramson (MURP 2027) interviews Christopher Pina, a Community Planner for the City of LA. They discussed how communities influence the planning activities one does, the differences between what is learned in school and what is carried out in professional planning, and whether theory serves as an important guide when taking courses of action.

Interview with Graham Rossmore, LADOT Parking Meters Division

Oscar Alanis-Perez (MURP 2027) interviews Graham Rossmore, a Transportation Planning Associate II at the LADOT Parking Meters Division. They discussed the high cost of free parking, al fresco dining, active transportation safety, and the importance of open deliberation to resolve conflicts.

Episode 3 · Fall 2025

Interview with Michelle Rivera, Grassroots Planner

Marciel Andrade (MURP 2027) interviews Michelle Rivera, a grassroots planner working with Public Matters and the Thai Community Development Center. They discussed bilingual outreach, effective community engagement, political and financial constraints, and co-production to build and learn from residents' skills and knowledge.
